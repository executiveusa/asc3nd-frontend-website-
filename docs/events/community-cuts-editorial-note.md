# Editor’s Note: Event Naming Hierarchy

## Decision

- **Community Cuts for Kids** is the campaign and recurring event-series name.
- **Fresh Fade, Fresh Grade** is the 2026 Everett edition line.

This is an editorial hierarchy, not a silent rename.

## Why this preserves clarity

The campaign artwork and earlier public references already teach people to look
for `Community Cuts for Kids`. Replacing that name on the QR destination would
make the landing page feel disconnected from the flyer that brought the visitor
there.

`Fresh Fade, Fresh Grade` is strong campaign language, but it does a different
job: it gives the 2026 edition energy and a memorable back-to-school promise.
Keeping it beneath the stable event-series name lets ASC3ND refresh future
editions without rebuilding recognition from zero.

The decision follows three established usability principles:

1. Steve Krug’s core guidance is to reduce avoidable thinking. A flyer and
   landing page that use the same primary event name create immediate
   recognition.
2. Nielsen Norman Group’s consistency-and-standards heuristic supports using
   the same label for the same thing across channels.
3. Jakob’s Law recognizes that people carry expectations from one touchpoint to
   the next. The QR destination should confirm, not contradict, the source.

These principles support clarity; they do not guarantee conversion performance.
The hierarchy should be changed only if ASC3ND explicitly approves a full
campaign rename and updates the flyer, social posts, QR references, and partner
materials together.

## Implementation rule

Use the names in this order:

1. `Community Cuts for Kids`
2. `Fresh Fade, Fresh Grade`
3. Date, time, venue, and participation action

Do not present the two names as competing event titles.

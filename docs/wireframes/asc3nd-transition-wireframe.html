Why ASC3ND exists

Every young person deserves a meaningful next step.

Founded by Otha and Elisha Minnifield, The ASC3ND Collective connects young people with mentorship, experiences, and community support that help confidence become direction.

One caring adult. One real opportunity. One community willing to show up.

Building forward

The event becomes a path.

ASC3ND is using what the community learned and created together to shape the next stage of its work. These pathways remain directional until the founders approve the formal program model.

01

Mentorship and trusted guidance

Young people need consistent adults who listen, encourage, and help them recognize what is possible.

02

Leadership and life skills

Practical experiences can turn confidence into responsibility, resilience, and direction.

03

Community programs and partnerships

Families, schools, mentors, businesses, and neighbors can create opportunities no single organization could build alone.

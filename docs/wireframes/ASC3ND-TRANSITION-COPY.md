- Mode: brownfield transition and rebrand

## Permanent site architecture

1. `/` - mission, current proof, and primary participation action
2. `/story` - founders, origin, belief, and community context
3. `/programs` - verified programs only
4. `/impact` - Community Cuts case study, future results, and approved stories
5. `/events` - past events and future opportunities
6. `/get-involved` - family, volunteer/mentor, partner/sponsor, and donor paths
7. `/es` - intentionally translated Spanish experience
8. `/contact` - short contact path and organizational details
9. `/privacy` - privacy policy
10. `/youth-safety` - safeguarding and media-consent practices
11. `/staff` - protected staff access; never primary navigation

## Visual rejection rules

- No generic AI-generated youth or community photography
- No gradient-led identity
- No rainbow palette or competing accent colors
- No emoji icons
- No repeated rounded card grid
- No raster logo or text baked into images
- No filler statistics
- No claims of impact without evidence
- No decorative motion
- No compressed-desktop mobile layout
- No technical operating-system language on the public path

## Production evidence still required

- Final event attendance and youth-served count
- Volunteer, barber, sponsor, and partner count
- Approved event photographs and media releases
- Confirmed program names and status
- Confirmed contact, social, donation, and newsletter destinations
- Founder-approved biography and organizational status
- Accessibility, mobile, performance, security, form, and rollback verification
